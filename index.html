Selected research

Enlarge image

Human–AI interaction

Theseus

Theseus is a graph-based workspace where researchers and AI agents break down hypotheses, review experiment protocols, interpret results, and decide what to investigate next. Proposed changes stay visible and reviewable before they become part of the workspace.

Enlarge image

AI for science

HypoMap-Bench

A benchmark that tests how well language models audit, repair, and extend hypothesis maps built from research papers. Models have to judge what the evidence supports, find gaps, and propose a justified next step.

Enlarge image

Lab automation

MARS

MARS (Modular Agentic Robot Scientist) connects a planning conversation with a digital twin of the lab bench. Language models propose a protocol; deterministic code validates and schedules it against the actual layout.

Enlarge image

Multi-agent systems

Reliable robot teams

A handoff can sound correct while pointing at the wrong object. This work studies silent reference failures in teams of language-model-driven robots, using a simulated testbed to trace where evidence is introduced, lost, or misread.
